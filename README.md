The Sales Performance Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence dashboard created using **Google Looker Studio**.

The purpose of this project is to analyze sales, profit, orders, quantity, product categories, geographical performance, and order priorities through interactive visualizations and KPIs.

This dashboard converts raw sales data into meaningful business insights that can help organizations understand their overall sales performance and identify areas of improvement.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Monitor monthly sales and profit trends
- Analyze sales by category and sub-category
- Understand country-wise sales performance
- Analyze order priority distribution
- Calculate important business KPIs
- Provide interactive filtering for business analysis
- Identify high-performing and low-performing areas

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Google Looker Studio** – Dashboard development and visualization
- **Microsoft Excel / CSV** – Data source
- **Calculated Fields** – KPI calculations
- **Google Maps** – Geographical sales analysis
